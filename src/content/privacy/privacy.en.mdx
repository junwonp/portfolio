# Privacy Policy

This portfolio website respects and protects the privacy of its visitors.

### 1. Automatically Collected Information
The following information may be automatically generated and collected during your visit:
- **IP Address (Client IP Address)**
- **Country / Geolocation**
- **Browser Information (User Agent)**
- **Referrer (Previous page link)**
- **Service Usage Metrics** (Dwell time, scroll progress, etc.)

### 2. Purpose of Collection and Use
The collected information is used solely for the following purposes:
- **Security & Website Protection**: Blocking automated bot scanning, detecting abnormal traffic, and preventing security threats.
- **Service Improvement**: Analyzing anonymous traffic patterns to optimize performance and portfolio usability.

### 3. Retention Period
Visitor IP address logs and session data are stored temporarily for security and analysis purposes and will be permanently deleted from the database after a period of up to 30 days.

### 4. Third-Party Sharing and Marketing
We do not use any collected visitor data for marketing or profiling purposes, and we never share, rent, or sell your information to any third parties.
